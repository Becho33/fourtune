My story

Guided by the heart,
grounded in tradition

A calling, quietly discovered

My connection began
through family

I am Japanese, and some of the spiritual knowledge I use today was first introduced to me through my grandmother.

What she passed down was more than a collection of techniques. It encouraged me to notice intuition, emotion and the subtle messages we sometimes experience throughout life.

As I grew older, my own moments of spiritual guidance taught me to trust my heart more deeply. I realised that I wanted to use what I had learned to help others find clarity, reassurance and a different perspective.

My philosophy

Insight, never instruction

I believe intuition can be a powerful companion to logic.

A reading should never take away your independence or claim that your future is completely fixed. It is another way to explore your experiences, recognise patterns and consider possibilities.

Your choices always remain your own.

My dream is simple

To help people find clarity, hope and direction during the moments when they need it most.
